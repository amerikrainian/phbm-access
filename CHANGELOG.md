# Changelog

## V0.0.5

- The game really lacks audio sliders. This is our attempt at fixing this by adding master, music, and sfx volume sliders to the game's options.

## V0.0.4

- In combat, numbers 1 through 4 can now be used as shortcuts for your left/right hands and hero skills, respectively.

## V0.0.3

- Added ctrl+tab to switch between shop and equipment views.

## V0.0.2

- We now pick up in-game chatter, with the option to turn idle chatter off in settings.

## V0.0.1

- Initial release.
